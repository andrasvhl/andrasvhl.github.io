Improve Product

A design council that tests its own work.

Four roles, or six, design a change to your product from how people actually used it, agree one model, build it, then use it for real in a sandbox until nothing serious is left.

Download Improve Product ↓ Free · MIT licence · README inside
01

What it does

Stories from real use

Reads your logs and chats for what people were trying to get done and what it cost them, then designs for the goal, not the old clicks.

Small or Full council

Systems, product, UI and copy; Full adds a QA engineer and a behavioural psychologist, at a lower vote you can change.

Tested hands-on

Each role clicks, sends and submits in a sandbox where nothing reaches a real person, one at a time on fresh data.

Iterates to done

Findings come back in numbered tiers. Fix, test again, up to three rounds, then you approve the deploy.

02

How it works

  1. Say “run the council on the settings screen” and pick Small or Full.
  2. The first run finds your usage data and walks you through setting up a sandbox.
  3. You approve one proposal, with every behaviour change listed so you can veto line by line.
  4. The roles build, test, fix and test again, then hand you the deploy.
03

What you need

  • Claude Code
  • A product you can run locally or on a server you control
  • Optional: a browser tool such as Claude in Chrome, for hands-on rounds
